3. VERIFY THE RELEASE, NOT JUST THE CONFERENCE CALL

Verify issuer identity, fiscal period, calendar year/date, timezone and the actual results-release window using issuer IR announcements/filings. Preserve source URL and publication, observation and receipt timestamps. Store results release and conference call separately. A call at 16:30 or 17:00 does not itself prove an AMC release.

- Confirmed AMC: plan entry before that session's regular close and exit at the next regular opening.
- Confirmed BMO: plan entry before the immediately preceding session's close and exit at the release day's regular opening.
- Require the results release inside the intended close-to-next-open hold.
- Conflicting issuer/sheet dates, unknown timing/year, already published results, rescheduling outside the hold or a release after our exit blocks the current proposal. Record the conflict; a corrected event needs a new frozen plan and review rather than an automatic date change.

Keep Tuesday-Thursday entries, review at close minus 30 minutes, entry target minus five and cutoff minus one. Derive actual sessions from the exchange calendar, including early closes, and display Europe/Paris summer-time-aware times. Do not hard-code CET or infer holidays from weekdays alone.
